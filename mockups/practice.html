My Practice

How do we work?

Individual sessions run 60–90 minutes, in person or remote. Team sessions are scoped during contracting. Depending on urgency, coaching contracts run in six-month intervals.

Who is a good fit

  • You're leading or managing genuine complexity
  • You see diversity of thought and perspective as potential for innovation, not a problem
  • You don't believe in "how it's supposed to be"
  • Your curiosity about yourself, and everyone else involved, is strong

When it's not a good fit

  • You want simple answers to complex questions
  • You're not willing to examine your own assumptions
  • You see coaching as something for "problem cases," or a precursor to the coachee leaving

The commitment to you

What working with Alluvial actually looks like.

Coaching that respects complexity

No oversimplification, no one-size-fits-all limitations.

A safe, thinking environment

Room to explore every perspective and idea, without judgement.

Deep listening, with humility

There is no single perfect answer, and I won't pretend there is.

Practical transformation actions

Changes that noticeably improve performance, not just how a problem feels.

Sustained impact

What you discover in a session is built to stay with you after it ends.

Introductory programmes

Two entry points, depending on where you're starting.

Sample of One™

For when you're not yet sure what the real issue is.

Builds self-awareness so you can respond better to change, by reframing difficulties and understanding that differences aren't necessarily blockers, or personal. Typically 4–6 sessions, depending on how many people are involved and how urgent it is.

How it runs

  • Session 1 — Chemistry and curiosity survey, with any scope adjustments
  • Session 2 — The scoped issue from your perspective, informed by a psychometric survey of your strengths and blind spots
  • Session 3 (if useful) — External feedback, including sessions with the people giving it
  • Session 4 — A personalised map of the problem and the options, built together

Most coachees reach an "ah-ha" moment somewhere in this process that changes how they approach the issue, and lets them move immediately.

How it runs

  • Session 1 — Chemistry review: do you believe you can work effectively together, and why was I engaged
  • Sessions 2–6 — Clarify the goal and the current reality, then iterate: what success looks like, what to experiment with, what the results show, and reframe

Ends in measurable improvement and observable change, not just a better feeling about the problem.

GROW into Solutions™

For when the issue is clear and you've coached before.

We address it directly. Together we explore solutions with curiosity about ourselves, non-judgemental acceptance, and a clear read on which of our natural ways of working help, or get in the way of, performance. Typically 2–6 sessions, depending on complexity and how many people are involved.

Within both programmes, you may be given articles, readings or thought exercises between sessions: never mandatory, always tailored to the issue at hand. Where a gap turns out to be a skill, not a coaching issue (new to sales, negotiation, project management, remote management, working across cultures), I'll say so rather than turn coaching into a substitute for your organisation's own learning and development.
